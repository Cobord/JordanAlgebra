# Trialities

A *triality* over a commutative star ring `R` is a trilinear form

```
t : M1 →ₗ[R] M2 →ₛₗ[starRingEnd R] M3 →ₗ[R] R
```

linear in `x ∈ M1` and `z ∈ M3`, antilinear in `y ∈ M2`, on nontrivial modules, such that fixing a
*nonzero* element in any one slot pairs the other two modules perfectly: each is carried
isomorphically onto the (conjugate) dual of the other (the six `bij_*` axioms). The model is the
octonion triality `t x y z = ⟨x * y, z⟩`, and the point of the definition is that a triality is the
same thing as a division algebra once two distinguished elements are chosen (Baez, *The
octonions*, §2.4).

The `bij_*` axioms are strong: they force every non-zero-divisor of `R` to divide every value of
`t`, so over a domain the nonzero values of `t` are units, and over a ring with a self-adjoint
idempotent `e` the triality lives entirely over the corner `eR` or over `(1 - e)R`. All the
examples therefore live over fields.

## Files

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [`Basic.lean`](Basic.lean) | The `Triality` class. Re-curryings `triality_prod_xz`, `triality_prod_yz`; the derived nondegeneracy `nondegen_xy/yz/zx` and injectivity of the outer curryings; the duality isomorphisms `xEquiv₂₃ x hx : M2 ≃ₛₗ M3ᵛ`, …, `zEquiv₂₁` for a nonzero element in one slot; `ofTrivialStar`, building a triality from an ordinary trilinear form when `star` is trivial. Structural consequences: every value `t x y z` lies in `aR` for every non-zero-divisor `a` (`triality_prod_mem_span`), so over a domain `t x y z = 0 ∨ IsUnit (t x y z)`; for an idempotent `e`, `M1 = eM1` or `M1 = (1 - e)M1` (`forall_smul_eq_self_or_forall_one_sub_smul_eq_self`). Also `Triality.Equiv`, isomorphisms of trialities over the same ring. |
| [`Corner.lean`](Corner.lean) | Reduction along a self-adjoint idempotent `e` (`star e = e`). The corner ring `Corner he hs = eR` with its inherited star and projection `proj : R →+* eR`; `IsCornerModule`, the compatibility of an `R`-module structure with an `eR`-module structure, under which `R`-duals and `eR`-duals coincide (`dualEquiv`, `conjDualEquiv`); `restrict` (a triality over `R` on modules fixed by `e` is one over `eR`) and `lift` (a triality over `eR` is one over `R`), on the same carriers via the synonyms `Restrict`/`Lift`. Main theorem `equiv_lift_or_equiv_lift`: every triality over `R` is isomorphic to the lift of its restriction to `eR`, or to the lift of its restriction to `(1 - e)R` — the Peirce argument of `Basic.lean` applied to all three modules, plus the fact that mixed components force `t = 0`. In particular a triality over `R₁ × R₂` is a triality over one factor. |
| [`DivisionAlgebra.lean`](DivisionAlgebra.lean) | Choosing nonzero elements in two of the three modules identifies both with the dual of the third and transports `t` to a product there: `DivAlg₁₂ = M3ᵛ` (from `e₁ ∈ M1`, `e₂ ∈ M2`), `DivAlg₁₃ = M̄2ᵛ`, `DivAlg₂₃ = M1ᵛ`, each a `NonAssocRing` and `R`-algebra with unit `t e e' ·`, and each a `DivisionAlgebra R` (left and right multiplication by nonzero elements bijective, straight from the `bij_*` axioms). The identifications `ofM1`, `ofM2`, … and the transport lemma `ofM1_mul_ofM2 : ofM1 x * ofM2 y = t x y`. |
| [`Gauged.lean`](Gauged.lean) | `GaugedTriality`: a triality with homogeneous gauges `Mᵢ → Γ` into an ordered commutative monoid `Γ` and a multiplicative gauge `R →* Γ`, bounding the product: `gauge (t x y z) ≤ gauge x * gauge y * gauge z`. `NormedTriality`: the gauges come from `R`-valued inner products (`InnerProductForm`: a sesquilinear form that is `LinearMap.IsPosSemidef` and definite) for a `StarOrderedRing R`, whose partial order has nonnegative cone generated by the `star s * s` (for `ℂ`: `z ≤ w` iff `w - z` is a nonnegative real). The bound reads `|t x y z|² ≤ ⟪x,x⟫ ⟪y,y⟫ ⟪z,z⟫` and is attained symmetrically in the three slots (Baez's condition: fixing any two of `x, y, z`, some nonzero value of the third gives equality), and the induced `GaugedTriality` has `Γ = {r : R // 0 ≤ r}`, the "nonnegative reals" of `R`. Also a division-free Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for nonnegative symmetric bilinear forms over a commutative star-ordered ring with trivial star (`cauchySchwarz_of_isUnit`), used by the octonion example. |
| [`Field.lean`](Field.lean) | Fields with involution as trialities. For a finite separable extension `K / F` of a star field `F` and a `star`-compatible automorphism `τ` of `K` (no involution on `K` itself needed — in `Triality` only the base ring carries a star), `t x y z = Tr_{K/F} (x * τ y * z)` is a `Triality F K K K` (`ofTrace`), with the perfect pairings coming from the trace form (`traceForm_nondegenerate`). Instances: `K = F`, `τ = star` (`t x y z = x * star y * z`); and finite fields `Fq ⊆ Fq2 ⊆ Fqn`, where `Fq2 = 𝔽_{q²}` carries the Frobenius involution `r ↦ r ^ q` (`frobeniusStarRing`) and `Fqn` is any finite extension of `Fq2`, with `τ` the `q`-Frobenius of `Fqn` (`finiteFieldTriality`). |
| [`Octonion.lean`](Octonion.lean) | The octonion triality `t x y z = ⟨x * y, z⟩` on `Octonion R a b c` (`Jordan/Octonion.lean`), over a commutative ring `R` with `2` invertible and trivial `star`, under anisotropy of the norm form (`x * star x` a unit for `x ≠ 0`) and perfectness of the polarized norm form (`innerProduct` onto the dual; automatic over a field by dimension count). Along the way `Octonion.divisionAlgebra : DivisionAlgebra R 𝕆`. Choosing `e = 1` in both slots, each of `DivAlg₁₂`, `DivAlg₁₃`, `DivAlg₂₃` is `𝕆` itself: `equivDivAlg₁₂/₁₃/₂₃ : 𝕆 ≃+*` the corresponding algebra (with the multiplicative identification being `ofM1`, `ofM3`, and the `star`-twisted `ofM2` respectively). For `R` a `StarOrderedRing` with nonnegative norm form, `innerProduct` is an `InnerProductForm` (`innerForm`) and the triality is a `NormedTriality` (`normedTriality`): the bound `⟨xy,z⟩² ≤ ‖x‖²‖y‖²‖z‖²` is Cauchy–Schwarz plus `‖xy‖² = ‖x‖²‖y‖²`, attained at `z = xy`, `y = x̄z`, `x = zȳ`. `normedTrialityOfSigns` takes the sign conditions `a ≤ 0`, `b ≤ 0`, `0 ≤ c` (Cayley's octonions) in place of nonnegativity of the norm form. |
